# Your phone or computer, inside Forge

Forge does not replace the system it runs on; it sits in front of it. In the lab there is a screen
that is your real phone or computer: walk up to it (or tap it) and your apps are on it. Open one and
it opens for real while Forge steps aside. Open Forge again and you are exactly where you were,
because Forge keeps its state. Nothing of your phone or computer is lost by living in Forge.

## Using it

- **Get to it:** the screen stands a few steps ahead and to the right of where you start. When you
  are close, **Use your phone** (or **Use your computer**) appears. Tap it or press **E**. Tapping the
  screen itself works too.
- **Open an app:** search, then tap the app (or press Enter for the first match).
  - On a computer, Forge minimizes itself.
  - On a phone, Forge goes to the background.
- **Coming back:** open Forge again from the taskbar, dock or recent apps.
- **From anywhere:** the **Phone** (or **Computer**) button above **Find room** opens your apps
  without walking to the screen. If the screen is ever gone (after Reset or the lab tour, say),
  Forge puts it back.

## Forge as your phone's home screen (Android)

The phone's screen in Forge has **Use Forge as home screen**. It opens Android's own home-screen
setting, where you choose Forge. From then on, unlocking the phone or pressing Home brings you back
into your world, and your apps are one tap away on the screen in it.

To go back, tap **Stop using Forge as home screen** and pick your old launcher.

Installing Forge never changes your home screen on its own: Android lists Forge as a home screen
only after you turn this on.

If you turned this on in Forge 0.6.0 and got a black screen: that version started a second copy of
Forge when you pressed Home. Update, then tap **Use Forge as home screen** again (the setting starts
over after the update). Pressing Home now brings back the Forge that is already running.

## What it can and cannot do

- **Linux:** apps come from the system's application list (including Flatpak and Snap).
- **macOS:** apps come from the Applications folders.
- **Windows:** apps come from the Start menu.
- **Android:** every app with a launcher icon, with its icon.
- **Only what's listed:** Forge opens only apps from those lists, so nothing the page asks for can
  start an arbitrary program.
- **Browser tab:** the web version has no apps to open, so the screen does not appear. Add `?apps=demo`
  to the address to try the screen with a made-up list.
- **iPhone:** Apple does not let apps list or open other apps this way, or replace the home screen.
- **Not yet:** a live picture of your real screen on the screen in the world, and using apps without
  leaving Forge. Desktop first, with your permission.
